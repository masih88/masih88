## Basics

### Ohm's law & power

*U, I, R, P – enter any two values* — `ohm`

```text
Enter exactly two of the four quantities; the others are calculated.
U = R · I    P = U · I = I² · R = U² / R
```

**Inputs:** Voltage U [V], Current I [A], Resistance R [Ω], Power P [W]

**Results:** Voltage U [V], Current I [A], Resistance R [Ω], Power P [W]

### Power P, Q, S

*Active, reactive, apparent power from U, I, cos φ* — `power`

```text
S = k · U · I   (k = 1 for DC/1~, k = √3 for 3~ with line-to-line voltage)
P = S · cos φ    Q = S · sin φ    φ = arccos(cos φ)
```

**Inputs:** System, Voltage U [V], Current I [A], Power factor cos φ

**Results:** Apparent power S [kVA], Active power P [kW], Reactive power Q [kvar], Phase angle φ [°]

### Current from power

*I = P / (k · U · cos φ · η)* — `current_from_power`

```text
I = P / (k · U · cos φ · η)
k = 1 for DC/1~, k = √3 for 3~. P is the output power; use η = 100 % for input power.
```

**Inputs:** System, Power P [kW], Voltage U [V], Power factor cos φ, Efficiency η [%]

**Results:** Current I [A], Apparent input power S [kVA]

### Conductor resistance

*R = ρ(θ) · L / A* — `conductor_resistance`

```text
R = ρ(θ) · L / A    ρ(θ) = ρ20 · (1 + α20 · (θ − 20 °C))
Cu: ρ20 = 0.017241 Ω·mm²/m, α20 = 0.00393 1/K (IEC 60028)
Al: ρ20 = 0.028264 Ω·mm²/m, α20 = 0.00403 1/K (IEC 60889)
```

**Inputs:** Conductor material, Length L (one way) [m], Cross-section A [mm²], Conductor temperature θ [°C]

**Results:** Resistance of one conductor [Ω], Loop resistance (out + return) [Ω], Resistivity ρ(θ) [Ω·mm²/m], Conductivity κ(θ) [m/(Ω·mm²)]

### Voltage drop

*ΔU in V and %, losses, DE/CH limit* — `voltage_drop`

```text
ΔU = k · L · I · (ρ(θ)/A · cos φ + X' · sin φ)
k = 2 for DC/1~ (out and return), k = √3 for 3~ (line-to-line voltage)
ΔU % = ΔU / U · 100    P_loss = n · I² · ρ · L / A
Typically X' ≈ 0.08 mΩ/m for multi-core cables. 70 °C conductor temperature (PVC) is conservative; IEC 60364-5-52 Annex G uses ρ = 0.0225 Ω·mm²/m (Cu).
Limits: DIN 18015-1, DIN VDE 0100-520 Annex G, NIN – check against the edition in force and local rules.
```

**Inputs:** System, Nominal voltage U [V], Load current I_B [A], Cable length L (one way) [m], Cross-section A [mm²], Conductor material, Power factor cos φ, Conductor temperature θ [°C], Reactance per length X' [mΩ/m], Limit, Custom limit ΔU max [%]

### Disconnection / max. length (TN)

*Z_s,max and L_max for MCB B/C/D* — `disconnection`

```text
Disconnection in TN systems (IEC 60364-4-41, 411.4.4): Z_s · I_a ≤ U_0
Conventional method: L_max = 0.8 · U_0 · S_ph / (ρ · (1 + m) · I_a), m = S_ph / S_PE

I_a = instantaneous trip of MCB: B 5 · I_n, C 10 · I_n, D 20 · I_n. For fuses take I_a from the curve.
Does not replace measuring the loop impedance.
```
